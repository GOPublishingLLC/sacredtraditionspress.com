The Ethiopian Bible

The Ethiopian Orthodox canon is the oldest and most complete in the world — ninety books where most Bibles carry sixty-six. Enoch is here. So are Jubilees, Meqabyan and the rest of what the Western canon set aside. Annotated throughout, with the historical context for each text.

The English Bible of the Reformation, printed a half-century before the King James and carried to America by the Pilgrims. This is the full 1560 text with its original marginal notes — the commentary that made it the most politically dangerous book of its century.
